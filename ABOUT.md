# Clockshot

**Clockshot is a swinging race where time is your fuel.**

You start with ten seconds and one move: a grappling hook. There is no jump and
no weapon — the rope is the whole game. Swing across the arena, grab clocks to
buy yourself more seconds, and reach the goal before the timer hits zero.

Clocks are worth **+2 seconds** and the single golden clock is worth **+5**.
Spikes and enemies cost you two. Nothing else can hurt you.

Every second still on the clock when you touch the goal is worth points, and so
is every anchor you swung from along the way — so the greedy run and the fast
run pull against each other. Go wide and collect everything, or go straight and
arrive rich. Not reaching the goal scores nothing at all; a near miss is still
a miss.

Run out of time and you go straight back in. No death screen, no button. Green
flags along the route are checkpoints, so you pick up where you fell with the
clock you had when you passed them.

## One course at a time, for everybody

Three arenas ship — **The Gantry**, **The Well** and **The Span** — and every
ten minutes the game picks the next one for the whole subreddit at once. For
those ten minutes every player who opens the post is racing the exact same
course, so the name above you on the leaderboard actually earned its spot. Each
window has its own board, its own live feed of who just finished, and the
previous window's winner is still up when the new one starts.

## Build your own

**BUILD A LEVEL** opens an editor on a starter arena that already works. Lay
down floor, anchors, clocks, checkpoints, spikes and enemies on a 30 × 25 grid
against a fixed budget, then press **TEST** and play it. A level only reaches
your shelf once you have finished it yourself, so nothing on the shelf is
impossible. Built levels play through exactly the same code as the shipped
ones, and they are yours — kept on your own device, not posted anywhere.

Runs and builds both take over the whole screen, in the Reddit app and out of
it.

**How far can you swing before the clock runs out?**
