# Clockshot

**Clockshot is a swinging race where time is your fuel.**

You start with ten seconds and one move: a grappling hook. Swing across the
arena, grab clocks to buy yourself more seconds, and reach the goal before the
timer hits zero. Spikes and enemies cost you two seconds. Nothing else can hurt
you.

Every second still on the clock when you touch the goal is worth points — so
the greedy run and the fast run pull against each other. Go wide and collect
everything, or go straight and arrive rich.

Run out of time and you go straight back in. No death screen, no button. Green
flags along the way are checkpoints, so you pick up where you fell.

Every ten minutes the arena changes for everyone at once. For those ten
minutes, every player who opens the post is racing the exact same course — so
the name above you on the leaderboard actually earned its spot.

**How far can you swing before the clock runs out?**
